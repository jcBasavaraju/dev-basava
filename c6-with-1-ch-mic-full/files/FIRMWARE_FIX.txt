IMPORTANT FIRMWARE FIX

Inside mic_tester_firmware.ino

Find:

sendStatus("PLAY_TONE");

Replace with:

sendStatus("PLAY_TONE");
delay(5000);
sendStatus("RECORDING");

This allows the tone to play continuously for 5 seconds
before FFT recording starts.

Without this delay:
- tone plays once
- ESP32 records too early
- FFT becomes unstable
- test fails
